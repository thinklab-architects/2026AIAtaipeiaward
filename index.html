2026 AIA Taipei Design Awards — Call for Entries

The Program

Call for Entries

AIA Taipei, an international section established under AIA International to connect local members more closely with the global community of the American Institute of Architects, is pleased to announce the AIA Taipei Design Awards program.

The program aims to celebrate design excellence by recognizing outstanding architects whose built works exemplify architectural innovation and contextual sensitivity, while promoting their distinguished contributions to contemporary architectural discourse.

Awards will be presented across three categories based on scale: Small, Medium, and Large. The jury may select any number of winners in each category to honor exceptional works that have significantly impacted the advancement of architectural practice in Taiwan.

01 — Award Categories

Categories

All entries must be submitted according to the following 3 categories, based on total floor area:

Small
< 500 m²less than 500 m² of total floor area
Medium
500–3,000 m²from 500 m² to 3,000 m² of total floor area
Large
> 3,000 m²greater than 3,000 m² of total floor area
The project must be built work completed on or after 1 January 2021.
02 — Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility

AIA members and licensed architects who are in good standing are eligible to submit entries, based on the following project location requirements:

Who may enter

  • AIA Taipei Architect Members in good standing
  • Other AIA Members in good standing — membership active at time of submission
  • International Licensed Architects — licensed, non-AIA members
  • The entrant must be the primary author of the design submitted
  • Individuals may submit multiple entries, with no category restriction

Project location

  • Projects located in Taiwan, or
  • Projects designed by Taiwan-based architects

04 — Submission Requirements

How to Submit

For each project, the following submission materials must be uploaded to a web-based file transfer platform (e.g., Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive, WeTransfer, etc.) as a ZIP file. Please share the download link via email.

The submission ZIP file (100 MB max) should include:

  • Entry Form (Appendix A)
  • Project Description Form (Appendix B)
  • Images (10–15, JPEG, 200–300 dpi)
  • Portfolio PDF (up to 15 pages, A3 landscape, anonymous)
  • Copyright Release Form (Appendix C)
  • Submission Checklist Form (Appendix D)
  • Photographer Permission Form (Appendix E)
  • Proof of Entry Fee Payment
